Build a modern, highly polished React marketing landing page for a health and fitness tracking app called "CatchUp". Use Vite, React, and plain CSS. Please install and use react-icons for the icons.

The Vibe & Styling:
The design should feel like a mix of Apple Health and Duolingo—friendly, clean, and approachable. Use large border-radiuses (e.g., 24px to 40px) and glassmorphism effects for the cards.

Color Palette:

Main Background: Soft warm peach (#EAC4B3)

Primary Action Color: Vibrant Tomato Red (#DE4B4E)

Text Colors: Deep dark gray for headings (#111111), medium gray for body text (#666666)

Card Backgrounds: Pure white (#FFFFFF) or frosted glass (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6) with a backdrop-filter blur)

Please structure the single-page app into these 4 sections:

1. Navigation Bar

Layout: Flexbox, space between.

Left side: A logo consisting of a tomato emoji 🍅 and the text "Marketing" in a bold font.

Right side: A plain text "Log In" button and a frosted glass pill-shaped "Get Started" button.

2. Hero Section

Layout: 2-column split (Left text, Right visual).

Left side: A massive headline: "Catch up with your best self." A subtitle: "Sync your Apple Health, close your daily rings, and compete with friends. Your all-in-one health dashboard for steps, sleep, vitals, and more." A large pill-shaped primary CTA button in Tomato Red saying "Start Tracking for Free". Underneath, tiny text: "Available in English, 中文, and Bahasa Melayu."

Right side (Visual Mockup): Create a tilted, floating frosted glass card. Inside it, put a red Activity Ring icon, a bold title "Activity Ring", and text "80% Completed Today". Give this card a soft, deep drop shadow so it looks 3D.

3. Core Features Grid

Background: Make this section's background pure white (#FFFFFF) with a massive top-left and top-right border radius (e.g., 80px) so it swoops up over the peach background.

Header: Centered. Headline: "Everything you need to stay healthy." Subtitle: "We do the math. You do the moving."

Grid: A clean 3-column CSS grid. Create 5 feature cards. Each card needs a white background, a soft shadow, a 24px border radius, a colored icon from react-icons, a bold title, and a short description.

The 5 Features:

"Health Sharing & Leaderboards" (Two people icon). "Build your network, compete on the podium, and earn the Monthly Mover crown together."

"CatchUp Chatbot" (Message icon). "Have a health question? Chat with our smart AI assistant for personalized wellness tips."

"Biomarker PDF Reports" (Document icon). "Instantly download a beautifully formatted health report for your doctor."

"Automatic Syncing" (Refresh arrows icon). "Seamlessly pull data from your favorite wearables."

"Track All Vitals" (Heartbeat icon). "Monitor your blood pressure, sleep, heart rate, and water intake."

4. Bottom CTA & Footer

Background: Very light gray (#FCFCFC).

Content: Huge centered headline "Ready to close your rings?", subtitle "Join CatchUp today and start your journey to better health." A large Tomato Red button: "Create Your Account".

Footer: Centered gray text: "© 2026 CatchUp Health. All rights reserved."
